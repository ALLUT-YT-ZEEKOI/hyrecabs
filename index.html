Rental Knowledge Base

Everything you need to know about renting with HyreCabs.

Q1 What documents are required to rent a self-drive car/vehicle?
✕
Indian Nationals require an Original Driving License (held for at least 1 year) and an Aadhaar Card or Passport. Foreign Nationals require an International Driving Permit (IDP), country driving license, and passport copy.
Q2 When and how is the security deposit refunded?
+
The security deposit is refunded back to your original bank account/UPI within 7 working days after returning the vehicle cleanly.
Q3 What is the speed limit policy in Kerala?
+
All our vehicles are equipped with GPS monitoring with an 80 km/h speed limit advisory in accordance with Kerala MVD road safety rules.
Q4 What is the cancellation policy?
+
Cancellations made more than 24 hours prior to trip start receive a 100% full booking refund. Cancellations under 24 hours receive a 50% refund.
